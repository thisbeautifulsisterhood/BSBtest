        <h2>Educational Resources</h2>

        <p>
          <em>This Beautiful Sisterhood</em> Project provides readings,
          educational resources, and workshops to support educators in
          integrating the <em>Beautiful Sisterhood</em> collection into
          their classrooms.
        </p>

        <p>
          This curriculum demonstrates strategies for using the resources
          within our collection, including the digital archive and exhibits,
          and methods for contextualizing the 1884 World’s Fair within the
          context of the following themes: contextualizing the meaning of
          “woman” and 19th-century “womanhood,” gender, race, region/south,
          and sectionalism.
        </p>

        <p>
          Educational resources may include syllabi, units/modules,
          assignments, readings, multimedia, and/or teaching debriefs.
        </p>

      </div>
