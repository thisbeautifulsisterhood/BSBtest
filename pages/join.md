        <h2>
          Join the Sisterhood!
        </h2>

        <p>
          <em>This Beautiful Sisterhood of Books</em> is a collaborative
          space. Its purpose is to foster a community of scholars and readers
          working together to interpret and re-interpret an extraordinary –
          and all but forgotten – episode from women’s literary and cultural
          history.
        </p>

        <p>
          Our project contributes to the ongoing recovery of forgotten works
          by early women writers, and it provides a framework for exploring
          their significance to readers in 1884 and today. Here are some ways
          that you, your students, and your community members can Join the
          Sisterhood!
        </p>

      </div>

  <section class="bsb-join-contribute">

    <div class="bsb-join-contribute-inner">

      <h2>
        Contribute
      </h2>

      <p>
        We invite research and writing about Maud’s Beautiful Sisterhood of
        Books that examines questions about:
      </p>

      <ul>
        <li>its racial, class, and sectional politics;</li>
        <li>
          its importance as an episode in library, literary, and/or
          exposition history;
        </li>
        <li>introducing and interpreting the original archive;</li>
        <li>methods for expanding and updating the archive.</li>
      </ul>

      <p>
        We especially invite research and writing that asks questions we
        haven’t yet thought to ask. If you would like to contribute original
        research or assign research projects to your students,
        <a href="https://thisbeautifulsisterhood.org/contact-us/">
          contact us
        </a>
        to propose topics. We can provide guidelines and examples, and we
        welcome your suggestions.
      </p>


      <div class="bsb-join-accordion">

        <details>

          <summary>
            <span class="bsb-join-plus">+</span>
            Help us <em>build</em> the Beautiful Sisterhood
          </summary>

          <div class="bsb-join-panel">

            <p>
              We have not yet tracked down many items from Maud Howe’s
              catalogue. In some cases, we have not been able to verify that
              a particular text even exists. For those entries, we’re hoping
              that someone will recognize a title or author and send us the
              missing information.
            </p>

            <p>
              In other cases, we know a work exists but have not found a
              digitized copy. We hope readers can send links to digitized
              versions, scan physical copies, or help identify newly available
              digital versions.
            </p>

          </div>

        </details>


        <details>

          <summary>
            <span class="bsb-join-plus">+</span>
            Help us <em>beautify</em> the Beautiful Sisterhood
          </summary>

          <div class="bsb-join-panel">

            <p>
              Many items from Maude Howe’s catalogue are wonderful visual
              objects, with elaborately decorated covers and spines, or
              colorful frontispieces and illustrations. We seek help
              gathering images of authors, books, and pages that we can
              display throughout our website.
            </p>

          </div>

        </details>


        <details>

          <summary>
            <span class="bsb-join-plus">+</span>
            Help us <em>interpret</em> the Beautiful Sisterhood
          </summary>

          <div class="bsb-join-panel">

            <p>
              Our goal is to provide a short piece of researched writing for
              every item and author included in the 1884 Woman’s Literary
              Department. If you would like to contribute an exhibit label,
              or if you are a teacher who would like your students to create
              exhibit labels, contact us for guidelines and examples.
            </p>

          </div>

        </details>


        <details>

          <summary>
            <span class="bsb-join-plus">+</span>
            Help us <em>contextualize</em> the Beautiful Sisterhood
          </summary>

          <div class="bsb-join-panel">

            <p>
              The work of literary recovery is not complete without research
              that situates authors, texts, collections, and exhibitions
              within historical contexts.
            </p>

            <p>
              What should we make of the fact that the New Orleans World’s
              Fair Woman’s Department only accepted work from white women?
              What do we learn about this early women’s library by looking at
              it through the lens of other libraries or other World’s Fairs?
            </p>

          </div>

        </details>


        <details>

          <summary>
            <span class="bsb-join-plus">+</span>
            Help us <em>teach</em> the Beautiful Sisterhood
          </summary>

          <div class="bsb-join-panel">

            <p>
              The editors of <em>This Beautiful Sisterhood of Books</em>
              seek educational resources that demonstrate strategies for
              using the collection in classrooms.
            </p>

            <p>
              Resources may include syllabi, units/modules, assignments,
              readings, multimedia, and teaching debriefs.
            </p>

            <p>
              To propose an educational resource, submit a short proposal and
              bio through the
              <a
                href="https://tulane.co1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_09gg00GSSSi4LVY"
                target="_blank"
                rel="noreferrer noopener"
              >
                application
              </a>.
            </p>

          </div>

        </details>

      </div>

    </div>

  </section>
